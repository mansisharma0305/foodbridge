How it works

1

Donors post surplus

Add what is available, how much, where, and the pickup deadline, with handling notes.

2

NGOs request & get approved

Recipients search by food, area and category, then request. The donor approves and shares the pickup details.

3

Collect & confirm

Food is handed over, both sides confirm, and the impact dashboard records it in the right unit.
