Food-safety guidance

Important: FoodBridge is a coordination platform. It does not inspect, test or certify food, and listing a donation is not a statement by FoodBridge that the food is safe. Follow the food-safety laws and guidance that apply in your area (in India, FSSAI guidance and any local rules).

For donors

  • Only donate food that was prepared, stored and held under proper temperature and hygiene conditions, and that you would be comfortable eating yourself.
  • Record when the food was prepared or acquired, set a realistic pickup deadline, and add handling notes (e.g. keep refrigerated, consume within a few hours).
  • Declare allergens and ingredients where known. Use clean, covered, food-grade containers.
  • Cancel the listing if circumstances change. Never hand over food that has been left in the temperature danger zone too long.

For recipients

  • Ask about ingredients, allergens and storage, and check packaging, labels and dates at pickup.
  • Transport food safely (insulated or cold containers where needed) and serve it promptly.
  • Reject any item you have doubts about, and report concerns using “Report an issue”.

Never rely on appearance alone

Food can look, smell and taste normal and still carry harmful bacteria. Appearance is not evidence of safety. When in doubt, throw it out.
